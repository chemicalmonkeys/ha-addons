# Changelog

## 0.2.0
- Executes allowlisted commands from the owner app: lights on/off. Everything else stays read-only.

## 0.1.5
- Windows/shutters, presence and opening sensors; lights driven by relays.
- Discovery mode to propose the property definition.

## 0.1.4
- Per-property translation of source states (e.g. a night scenario reported as a bypass).
- Alarm reports bypassed zones.

## 0.1.3
- Reloads the property definition when it changes in the control plane (no restart needed).

## 0.1.2
- Reports its version and deployment shape to the control plane (shown in Higgins Operations).

## 0.1.1
- Fix: the add-on can read its options.

## 0.1.0
- First release: read-only bridge from Home Assistant to Higgins.
